Kai Qiao

I am Kai, a first-year master's student at CMU's Robotics Institute. I completed my undergraduate studies at SUSTech, where I was advised by Prof. Chengzhi Hu and Prof. Jian Dai, working on full-stack robotic system development and perception using novel sensors. I also visited UC Berkeley's ICON Lab for half year, working on multi-agent coordination in a dual-arm robotic setup.

I am broadly interested in robotics, particularly in developing embodied intelligence that enables AI to interact with the physical world. My current research focuses on hierarchical robot intelligence (System 0/1/2 architectures), integrating high-level reasoning with low-level control for humanoid robots, and autonomous semantic navigation.
